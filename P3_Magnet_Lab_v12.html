2. Magnetic Poles and Strength
The magnetic force is strongest at the poles and becomes weaker towards the middle of a bar magnet.
Drag the paper clips to the circles before releasing them. Hang the paper clips on the various parts of the magnet and determine at which part of the magnet is the strongest and weakest.

Drag either pole to rotate the magnet to any direction. Then press Release Magnet and watch how it gradually settles.
